About / Mission

Guiding utilities through the digital grid evolution — from the control room floor to future-ready modernization strategy.

I bring over 16 years of experience in the electric industry, with a strong focus on Smart Grid technologies. For six years I served as a Control Room System Engineer in the G&T sector, gaining first-hand insight into how operators handle complex, high-stakes scenarios.

Within Schneider Electric's SME team, I contributed to the ADMS/DERMS product organization — playing a pivotal role in the development, implementation, and deployment of EcoStruxure solutions for customers worldwide.

Today I provide strategic consulting to utility clients: selecting optimal solutions, facilitating technology migrations, and shaping Smart Grid modernization strategy, while forging partnerships with the industry's leading vendors.
